# Design Context — Smashin Bakes

**Target audience**: Local residents of Barrhead/East Renfrewshire (Scotland) browsing on mobile most of the time, plus existing Facebook/Instagram followers moving over from social. Mostly women 20s-40s buying treats for themselves, gifting, or ordering for parties/events. Not tech-savvy shoppers — the UX needs to be obvious, not clever.

**Use cases**:
- Browse the catalog and drool over photos before deciding what to pre-order.
- Pick a pickup day (Fri/Sat) and place a pre-order (no payment online yet, v1).
- Return customers logging in to reorder favourites.
- Staff (non-technical) logging into a CMS to add/edit products, mark items on sale/new, and manage stock — this side should be plain, fast, and unglamorous by comparison.

**Brand personality/tone**: Boutique patisserie meets playful pop-culture novelty. The bakery's actual products have cheeky names ("Calvin Harris Cupcake", "99 Flake Cupcake") riffing on candy brands and pop culture — the brand is indulgent and fun, not stuffy or corporate-luxury. Think: a premium independent bakery that doesn't take itself too seriously. Warm, appetite-triggering, hand-crafted feel — not minimal/austere, not neon/gimmicky.

**Palette direction**: warm cream base, blush pink, terracotta/rust accent, deep chocolate-brown text, soft gold accent — already expressed in the placeholder category imagery at `static/images/placeholder/*.svg`.

**Constraints**: SvelteKit + Tailwind v4, adapter-node, must run well on modest shared hosting (keep JS light, no heavy animation libraries). Accessible (real customers, real business) — good contrast, readable type, obvious tap targets on mobile since most traffic will be phones.

**Differentiation goal**: should read as a real, distinctive independent-bakery brand — not a generic Bootstrap-y e-commerce template, not a cold minimalist SaaS aesthetic. The client's explicit ask: "a fantastic design that other cake and bakery websites would be jealous of."

**Admin/CMS**: separate, plainer visual register — clean, functional, unfussy (utility tool for staff), reusing the same base palette/type but with none of the storefront's flourish.
